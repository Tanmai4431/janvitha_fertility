## 💎 3. Core Services & Specialties

1. **Female Infertility:** Comprehensive medical evaluation, hormonal diagnostics, ovulation disorders, and tailored treatment plans for women.
2. **Male Infertility:** Detailed semen analysis, sperm function assessments, and advanced medical solutions for male-factor fertility concerns.
3. **IVF (In Vitro Fertilization):** Advanced assisted reproductive technology with egg retrieval, precision fertilization, and embryo transfer.
4. **IUI (Intrauterine Insemination):** Gentle, minimally invasive procedure placing concentrated prepared sperm directly into the uterus during ovulation.
5. **ICSI (Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection):** Specialized micro-manipulation procedure injecting a single healthy sperm directly into a mature egg.
6. **PICSI (Physiological ICSI):** Advanced physiological sperm selection using hyaluronan binding to ensure optimal genetic integrity.
7. **Genetic Program:** Pre-implantation genetic testing and reproductive genetic screenings to support healthy pregnancy outcomes.
8. **Fertility Preservation:** Egg freezing, sperm cryopreservation, and embryo freezing to protect future parenthood options.
9. **Blastocyst Culture:** Extended state-of-the-art embryo incubation up to Day 5/6 for superior selection and higher implantation rates.

## ❓ 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. **When should I visit a fertility center?**
   Individuals or couples who have been trying to conceive for over a year (or six months if the female partner is over 35), or who have known medical/reproductive conditions, are encouraged to consult a specialist for early evaluation.

2. **What services are offered at Janvithas Fertility Center?**
   We offer full-spectrum reproductive healthcare including Female & Male Infertility Evaluations, IUI, IVF, ICSI, PICSI, Genetic Screening, Fertility Preservation, and Blastocyst Culture.

3. **Do both partners need to get evaluated?**
   Yes. Conception involves both partners equally. A comprehensive dual evaluation ensures an accurate diagnosis and the most effective personalized treatment pathway.

4. **Is fertility treatment affordable?**
   At Janvithas Fertility Center, we believe in transparent, affordable specialty healthcare for all, providing accessible guidance and structured treatment plans without hidden costs.
